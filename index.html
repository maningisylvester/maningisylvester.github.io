FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

How do I know this will work for my hotel?

Start with an audit (KES 18,000). You get a full diagnosis of what is working, what is not, and a clear action plan before committing to a retainer.

We already use Booking.com and Expedia. Do we still need this?

Especially then. OTAs take 15–25% per booking, so every direct booking saves you that commission. The goal is to reduce OTA dependence, not replace it overnight.

How long before I see results?

Paid ads show results within 2–4 weeks once campaigns are optimised. SEO and content take 2–3 months to build momentum. We are transparent about timelines from day one.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No long-term lock-in. Monthly retainers require 30 days notice to cancel. The results should be the reason you stay, not a contract.

Is ad spend included in the package fee?

No. The package fee covers strategy, management and reporting. Ad spend is paid directly by you to Google and Meta, and we recommend a budget based on your goals.

Do you work with hotels outside Nairobi?

Yes. Digital marketing works remotely. We work with properties across Kenya and beyond, serving clients locally and internationally.
